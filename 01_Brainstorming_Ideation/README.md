# 1. Brainstorming & Ideation

## Project Name
EduGenie

## Problem Statement

Students may face difficulties in understanding complex topics,
creating practice questions, summarizing learning materials,
and planning their learning activities.

## Ideas Considered

- AI Question Answering System
- AI Study Assistant
- AI Quiz Generator
- Personalized Learning Assistant

## Final Idea

EduGenie is an AI-powered educational assistant that helps students
with Question & Answer, Topic Explanation, Quiz Generation,
Content Summarization, and Learning Recommendations.

## Objective

The main objective of EduGenie is to provide students with
an interactive AI-based learning assistant.
